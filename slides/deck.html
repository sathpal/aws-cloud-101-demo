The hook

You tap a reel. 300 milliseconds later it plays. What just happened?

Your phone asks a computer

somewhere within ~40 ms of you, over the internet

That computer asks a database

which of the 6 billion reels should this person see next?

Storage streams the video

from a copy already parked near you, not from the creator's phone

Someone gets a bill

for a few microseconds of compute and a few kilobytes of transfer

Nobody at Instagram bought a computer for you. They rented a slice of one, for one third of a second. That is cloud computing.

Keep this to 3 minutes. The point is: computers you never see, rented by the millisecond. Everything today expands one of these four boxes.

1 · Introduction to cloud computing 0:10

Computing you rent over the internet, on demand, and pay for by use.

Servers, storage, databases, networking, and increasingly AI models. You don't own the hardware, you don't wait for delivery, and you stop paying the moment you stop using it.

NIST SP 800-145, translated into plain words

Make them repeat the sentence. Everything else is detail on "rent", "on demand", "by use".
1 · Introduction to cloud computing

A startup launches an app. 2005 versus today.

2005

  • Buy 4 servers at ₹4 lakh each. Wait 8 weeks.
  • Rent rack space, power, cooling, and a person to patch them.
  • Guess launch-day traffic. Guess low: the site dies. Guess high: money sleeps in a rack.
  • Want Mumbai and Singapore? Do it all twice.
vs

Today

  • One command. Server exists in 60 seconds.
  • Patching, power, cooling, hardware failures: the provider's problem.
  • Traffic doubles at 9 pm? Capacity doubles at 9 pm, and shrinks at 2 am.
  • Mumbai and Singapore is a drop-down menu.
The 8-week purchase order is the emotional core. Ask: how many of you would have launched a startup in 2005 with your own money?

Amazon built infrastructure to survive its own festival peaks. Then it rented it out.

YearWhat happened

The purpose of AWS, in one line: turn every piece of IT into a utility you rent. Point out the region physically nearest to where the audience sits.
2 · Fundamentals and purpose of AWS

Regions, availability zones, data centres

Most large countries have a region or two nearby; India, for example, has Mumbai (2016) and Hyderabad (2022). Choosing a region is choosing where your data physically sits, which laws apply, and how far the bits travel.

Exact counts change; say "30-plus regions, 100-plus zones, check the AWS global infrastructure page". Multi-AZ is the answer to "what if AWS goes down".

2 · Fundamentals and purpose of AWS

Shared responsibility: AWS guards the building, you guard the keys

AWS: security of the cloud

  • Physical data centres, guards, cameras
  • Hardware, networking, hypervisor
  • Managed service patching (DynamoDB, Lambda runtime)
  • Compliance certifications (ISO, SOC, RBI-relevant controls)
+

You: security in the cloud

  • Your IAM users, keys, and permissions
  • Which ports you open (we open only port 80 today)
  • Your data: encryption, backups, who can read it
  • Your code: an SQL injection is still yours

3 · Key uses and benefits 1:35

Why every company moved: the same six reasons you just watched

Speed

Minutes instead of months. The deploy took 90 seconds.

Elasticity

Up and down with demand. The blast: 200 requests, zero errors.

Cost

Operating expense, no idle hardware. Today: ₹0.

Global reach

Deploy next to your users. Mumbai, Singapore, Frankfurt: a drop-down.

Reliability

Multi-AZ by default. Your row lives in three data centres.

Managed services

Databases, queues, AI models as APIs. Nobody patched anything today.

Common uses: web and mobile backends · storage and backup · analytics and big data · machine learning · IoT · disaster recovery · dev and test environments · video streaming.

4 · Real-world applications

Who runs on this, and what they would say about today's demo

Netflix

Runs almost entirely on AWS. Deliberately kills its own servers (Chaos Monkey) to prove nothing breaks. Our "servers created" counter, industrialised.

JioHotstar

Record live-stream concurrency during Cricket World Cup and IPL finals, tens of millions of viewers at once, pre-scaled on AWS. Our blast, with six more zeros.

Zomato · Dream11

Indian apps whose traffic is lunch peaks and match-time peaks. Elasticity is the business model.

NASA JPL

Mars rover images processed and served to the public from S3-style storage.

Formula 1

The live "F1 Insights" graphics on TV are cloud analytics on car telemetry.

Moderna

Designed its COVID-19 vaccine candidate in days on rented compute instead of building a supercomputer.

All are publicly documented AWS customer stories; figures as stated in those case studies.

Ask which Indian apps they use daily and guess which run on a cloud. Nearly all do.
